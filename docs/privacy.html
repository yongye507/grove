Privacy Policy

Grove · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Overview

Grove is a focus timer that grows a tree while you stay present. We designed Grove to work without an account and without tracking you.

Data we store on your device

Focus sessions (time, tree species, tags, grown or wilted), your settings (default duration, leave-grace seconds), and whether you unlocked Lifetime are saved locally with Apple’s on-device storage. This data stays on your device unless you back up or transfer the device with your own Apple tools.

Data we do not collect

We do not run analytics SDKs, do not use advertising identifiers, do not sell personal data, and do not require an account or email. Grove does not send your focus history to our servers because we do not operate a Grove cloud for this app.

Purchases

Lifetime unlock is processed by Apple through StoreKit / App Store. Apple handles payment details under Apple’s privacy policy. Grove only receives an entitlement signal that you own the product so locked trees and Month/Year stats can unlock on this device (and restore on your other devices signed into the same Apple ID).

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Grove may schedule a local alert when a focus session is due to finish. Notification permission is optional and managed in iOS Settings.

Children

Grove does not target children for data collection. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the support email listed on Grove’s App Store product page.

隐私政策

Grove · 最后更新：2026年11月26日

概述

Grove 是一款专注计时器：你保持专注，树就会一点点长大。我们让 Grove 无需账号即可使用，也不会追踪你。

存储在你设备上的数据

专注记录（时长、树种、标签、已长成或已枯萎）、你的设置（默认时长、离开宽限秒数），以及你是否已解锁终身版，都通过 Apple 的设备端存储保存在本地。除非你使用 Apple 自己的工具备份或迁移设备，否则这些数据只会留在你的设备上。

我们不收集的数据

我们不使用任何分析 SDK，不使用广告标识符，不出售个人数据，也不要求账号或邮箱。Grove 不会把你的专注记录发送到我们的服务器，因为这款 App 没有运营任何 Grove 云服务。

购买

终身版解锁由 Apple 通过 StoreKit / App Store 处理。支付信息由 Apple 依据其隐私政策处理。Grove 只会收到“你已拥有该商品”的权益信号，用于在本设备上解锁锁定的树种以及月、年统计（并可在登录同一 Apple ID 的其他设备上恢复）。

通知

如果你允许通知，Grove 会在专注计时即将结束时安排一条本地提醒。通知权限是可选的，可在 iOS 设置中管理。

儿童

Grove 不以儿童为对象收集数据。本 App 不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

变更

如果本政策有变更，我们会更新“最后更新”日期。更新后继续使用即表示你接受修订后的政策。

联系我们

对本政策有疑问：请使用 Grove 在 App Store 产品页上列出的支持邮箱。
